# Mistral AI

## Overview

Mistral AI is a French artificial intelligence company that develops large language models, an AI assistant (Mistral Vibe, formerly Le Chat) and enterprise AI infrastructure. Mistral AI is headquartered in Paris, France, and is a privately held, venture-backed company. Mistral AI positions itself as Europe's leading independent frontier AI developer, with an emphasis on "sovereign" AI, open-weight models and deployments that customers can run under their own control. Mistral AI's stated mission is to "make frontier AI open to all, and together solve the world's hardest problems". By September 2026 Mistral AI had become the most highly valued AI start-up in Europe, following a €3 billion Series D round.

## Founding and leadership

Mistral AI was founded in April 2023 (registered 28 April 2023) in Paris by three French researchers: Arthur Mensch (previously at Google DeepMind), Guillaume Lample and Timothée Lacroix (both previously at Meta's AI research lab, where they worked on the original LLaMA models). Arthur Mensch is Mistral AI's Chief Executive Officer, Guillaume Lample is Chief Science Officer and Timothée Lacroix is Chief Technology Officer. According to Mistral AI's own "About" page, Mistral AI hired its first employee on 5 June 2023 and reached 100 employees on 30 September 2024. Mistral AI had roughly 350 employees in 2025 (reported by Wikipedia and press coverage of the September 2025 round); more recent headcount figures are third-party estimates rather than company disclosures. Mistral AI has made acquisitions, including Paris-based Koyeb (February 2026) and Austrian industrial-simulation start-up Emmi AI (May 2026).

## Funding, ownership and valuation

Mistral AI has raised capital in rapid succession since 2023:

- **June 2023 (seed):** about €105 million at a roughly €240 million valuation, led by Lightspeed Venture Partners, with Eric Schmidt, Xavier Niel and JCDecaux among backers.
- **December 2023 (Series A):** about €385 million at a valuation of around €2 billion, with Andreessen Horowitz, BNP Paribas and Salesforce.
- **February 2024:** Microsoft invested about $16 million (€15 million) alongside an Azure distribution partnership.
- **June 2024 (Series B):** about €600 million at a €5.8 billion valuation.
- **9 September 2025 (Series C):** €1.7 billion at a €17.1 billion post-money valuation, led by ASML (the Dutch lithography company), with DST Global, Andreessen Horowitz, Bpifrance, General Catalyst, Index Ventures, Lightspeed and NVIDIA. ASML's contribution was widely reported as €1.3 billion, giving ASML a stake of about 11%.
- **March 2026:** about $830 million in debt financing to build data centres (reported by TechCrunch and others).
- **8 September 2026 (Series D):** €3 billion (about $3.6 billion) at a €21 billion post-money valuation (about $24 billion), led by Samsung Electronics, co-led by the EQT-managed Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity, with Advent, BlackRock, the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, a16z, Nvidia and Salesforce Ventures. TechCrunch called it the largest equity round ever completed by a European technology company. (Earlier, in June 2026, Bloomberg had reported talks at "about €20 billion".)

Mistral AI revenue: Arthur Mensch said at Davos in January 2026 that Mistral AI expected to exceed €1 billion in revenue by the end of 2026. Annual recurring revenue was reported at about €300 million in September 2025 and more than €400 million in early 2026 (secondary press reports citing Mensch). Revenue in 2024 was reported at about $30 million. These revenue figures are reported, not audited.

## Products and distribution

Mistral AI sells access to its models through its own platform (Mistral AI Studio, formerly La Plateforme) and through cloud marketplaces including Microsoft Azure Foundry, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, IBM watsonx and others. Mistral AI's consumer and workplace assistant was called Le Chat from February 2024 until May 2026, when it was rebranded Mistral Vibe; it is available on web, iOS and Android on a freemium model, with a Pro tier at $14.99 per month. Le Chat reportedly reached one million downloads within 14 days of its February 2025 mobile launch. Mistral AI's current product line-up, per its website, includes Vibe (AI agent), Studio (build and test applications), Forge (train custom models) and Compute (infrastructure).

Mistral Compute, announced on 11 June 2025 with Nvidia, is a European AI cloud initially built on 18,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell chips at a data centre in Essonne, near Paris. On 21 July 2026 Microsoft and Mistral AI announced an expanded, multibillion-dollar strategic partnership under which Mistral AI deploys thousands of NVIDIA Vera Rubin GPUs in Europe, with Mistral Medium 3.5 available in Microsoft Foundry and Copilot Studio. Mistral AI plans to build 1 GW of compute capacity in Europe by 2030. Named Mistral AI customers include ASML, CMA CGM (a €100 million deal in April 2025), HSBC, TotalEnergies, Stellantis, BNP Paribas, SAP, AXA, Orange and the European Space Agency; Mistral AI also works with the French armed forces and with Helsing on defence AI.

## Research, safety and openness

Mistral AI is known for releasing open-weight models, most under the permissive Apache 2.0 licence (Mistral 7B, Mixtral, Mistral Small 3, Magistral Small, the Mistral 3 family including Mistral Large 3). Mistral AI has also used more restrictive licences for some models (the Mistral Research Licence and Mistral Non-Production Licence in 2024) and kept others proprietary (for example Mistral Medium 3), before releasing Mistral Medium 3.5 under a modified MIT licence with commercial-use restrictions. Mistral AI's research contributions include popularising sparse mixture-of-experts architectures in open models (Mixtral) and efficient small models. Mistral AI lobbied during the drafting of the EU AI Act and is a signatory to the EU general-purpose AI Code of Practice process; its public safety approach focuses on content moderation APIs and customer-controlled deployment rather than a published frontier-risk framework comparable to those of some US labs. Mistral AI's Large 3 model was optimised with NVIDIA, vLLM and Red Hat for open deployment.
